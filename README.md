# Coffee Chain Sales & Profitability Analysis Using Python ☕

## Project Overview
This project analyzes coffee chain sales, costs, and profitability data to identify the key factors impacting financial performance. Using Python, I cleaned and analyzed sales data to evaluate revenue trends, product performance, cost behavior, and market profitability.

The goal of this analysis was to determine why the company exceeded sales expectations but fell short of profit targets.

---

## Business Problem
The coffee chain experienced strong sales growth but did not achieve expected profit goals. This analysis investigates whether rising production costs, product performance, or market differences contributed to lower profitability.

Key business questions:
- Did actual sales exceed budget expectations?
- Which product categories contributed most to costs?
- How did COGS impact overall profitability?
- Which markets generated the strongest performance?
- What actions could improve profit margins?

## Key Findings

### Sales Growth Did Not Translate Into Profit Growth
- Actual sales exceeded budget expectations, showing strong customer demand.
- Profit performance remained below target due to increasing production costs.

### Rising COGS Reduced Profitability
- COGS exceeded budget for 3 out of 4 product categories.
- Increased costs were the primary driver behind lower profit margins.

### Market Performance
- Major Markets generated the highest sales volume.
- Major and Small Markets maintained similar profit margins, indicating that cost increases—not weak sales—were the main profitability challenge.

## Conclusion
This analysis demonstrates how Python can be used to transform raw business data into meaningful insights. The findings show that improving cost control and operational efficiency are critical for increasing profitability while maintaining strong sales performance.
- Improve supplier negotiations and purchasing strategies to reduce COGS.
- Optimize inventory management to control production expenses.
- Prioritize marketing and resources toward high-performing markets.
- Monitor sales, COGS, and profit metrics regularly to identify cost issues early.
